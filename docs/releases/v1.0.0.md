# Space Shooter v1.0.0

First release. A 60-second polygon space shooter for the Miyoo Mini / Mini Plus under OnionOS, in the style of Starfox, with Mixtape's amber-phosphor look.

- Shoot the cyan drones (+100) and gold cubes (+300); chain quick hits for up to x4.
- Asteroids, pylons and beams can't be shot. Touch one and the ship explodes.
- Survive the full 60 seconds for a +1000 bonus and an S/A/B/C/D rank.
- Top-5 score table, music that can be toggled, pause menu.
- Synthesised sound effects and a synthwave loop, played through OnionOS's audio.

Built by Claude, Anthropic's AI model, for snyderman3000.
